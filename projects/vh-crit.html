03 / VR + AI

VH-CRIT

A virtual space to rehearse the decisions that matter during a radiological emergency.

CONTEXTU.S. Department of Energy internship project
ROLESimulation development
TOOLSUnity · generative AI

THE PROBLEM

Traditional disaster training can require large teams, extensive coordination, and significant cost. VH-CRIT offers another path: a controlled VR environment where trainees can build situational awareness and practice realistic dialogue.

THE EXPERIENCE

  • Trainees enter a high-stakes radiological crisis scenario in VR.
  • AI-driven first responders create real-time conversation and coordination challenges.
  • The system guides users through IAEA response procedures.
  • Decisions are evaluated against established best practices.

THE GOAL

Make emergency-manager training more accessible and repeatable while keeping the pressure, communication, and decision-making of a realistic scenario.
