04 / AI + EMBEDDED SYSTEMS

Animatronic
Talking Head

An ongoing experiment in giving a physical character a responsive voice and face.

STATUSWork in progress
COMPUTEJetson Orin Nano
SYSTEMSLocal LLM · speech pipeline · mechanics

THE IDEA

This project transforms a repurposed plush head into an animatronic character capable of real-time, AI-driven interaction. It is intentionally a mix of soft toy, embedded computer, mechanical build, and conversational interface.

THE STACK

  • A Jetson Orin Nano handles the onboard compute.
  • A local large language model provides conversational intelligence.
  • Speech-to-text processes incoming audio.
  • Low-latency text-to-speech produces the response.
  • A coordinated mechanism turns speech into jaw movement.

WHAT I’M EXPLORING

The challenge is not only getting each part to work; it is making the full interaction feel immediate and coherent. Timing, sound, response quality, and movement all have to support the same character.
