01 / AR + PHYSICS EDUCATION RESEARCH

Transit Method Setup

A physical solar system model, motorized and wired up, so students can watch a transit happen instead of reading its graph.

CONTEXTDAAD RISE Germany research internship, Ludwig Maximilian University of Munich · May–August 2026
ROLEXR education research intern — hardware, controls, and AR development
TOOLSUnity (C#) · AR Foundation · ARKit · Raspberry Pi · Python · SolidWorks · KiCad

THE PROBLEM

The transit method is how most known exoplanets were found: a planet passes in front of its star, and the star's brightness dips by a fraction of a percent. In a lecture that idea arrives as a light curve on a slide — a dip in a line graph — and the connection back to the physical geometry causing it is left to the student to make. The department already had a physical solar system model, but it was static, and nothing tied it to the data students were being asked to interpret.

THE APPARATUS

  • Adapted the existing physical model into a motorized apparatus driven by closed-loop stepper motor control.
  • Built a Python UDP server on a Raspberry Pi to stream live motor position out of the rig continuously.
  • Designed custom motor mounts, drive adapters, and a control PCB in SolidWorks and KiCad so the new hardware fit inside the model that already existed.

THE AR LAYER

  • Developed the AR application in Unity (C#) using AR Foundation and ARKit image tracking, registering virtual orbital data directly onto the physical model through an iPad.
  • Consumed the Raspberry Pi's UDP position stream in Unity, keeping the mechanical state of the apparatus and its virtual overlay synchronized in real time.
  • Integrated Philips Hue lighting over the CLIP v2 API to dim the room lighting in step with each planetary transit — the brightness drop students are learning to read, happening around them.

THE STUDY

The apparatus exists to answer a research question, not just to demonstrate one. It supports user studies with more than 30 student participants, evaluating how immersive tools affect physics learning outcomes.
