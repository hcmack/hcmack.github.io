PORTFOLIO / 2026

Henry Mack — portfolio

Hi, I’m Henry. I’m a mechanical engineering student at UCF, drawn to space, themed entertainment, and immersive engineering. I’ve interned at NASA Kennedy Space Center, GE Aerospace, LMU Munich, and UCF’s Institute for Simulation and Training. The work I care about sits at both ends of one idea — simulations that let people rehearse dangerous work safely, and experiences built purely to delight. Both are about putting someone somewhere they couldn’t otherwise go.

Transit Method Setup

A motorized star and planet at LMU Munich, paired with an iPad AR app. The planet orbits, the app draws the light curve dipping in real time, and a Hue strip inside the star shifts wavelength so students can see how exoplanet atmospheres are read.

Unity · ARKit · Raspberry Pi · Philips Hue · stepper control · SolidWorks · KiCad
